Behind Miniature Paper Worlds

Hi, I'm Ronald.

I love creating miniature worlds from paper. What started with simple printable shoebox projects gradually grew into a collection of habitats, dioramas and all kinds of miniature experiments.

For me, much of the fun is in figuring out how an idea can actually work: researching, measuring, testing, printing, cutting and changing things until a flat sheet of paper becomes a tiny three-dimensional world.

Miniature Paper Worlds is where I share the finished projects, but also some of the ideas and stories behind their design.
